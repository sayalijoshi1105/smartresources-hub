About the Scheme

The PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme aims to provide collateral-free, risk-free education loans up to ₹10 Lakhs to meritorious students for higher education. The scheme seeks to remove financial barriers and empower students to pursue their academic dreams at reputed institutions in India and abroad.

Key Highlights

Collateral-free loans up to ₹10 Lakhs

Available through partner banks

Interest subvention for eligible students

Supports study in India and abroad

Covers UG, PG and professional courses

Part of the National Education Policy (NEP) initiative

Eligibility Criteria

  • Admission secured to a NIRF-ranked or accredited higher education institution.
  • Enrolled in an undergraduate, postgraduate, or professional degree programme.
  • Indian citizen with valid Aadhaar-linked identity documents.
  • No existing collateral-backed education loan for the same course.

Benefits

  • Loan amount up to ₹10 Lakhs with no collateral or guarantor required.
  • Interest subvention of 3% during the moratorium period for eligible income groups.
  • Flexible repayment beginning after course completion.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official PM Vidyalaxmi portal and register with your email.
  2. Fill in your academic and institution details.
  3. Upload required documents (admission letter, fee structure, income proof).
  4. Select a partner bank and submit your loan application.
  5. Track application status directly on the portal.

Important Dates

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis throughout the academic year. Partner banks may have their own internal processing timelines — check the official portal for the latest updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is collateral required for loans above ₹7.5 Lakhs?

No — loans up to ₹10 Lakhs under this scheme remain collateral-free for eligible institutions.

Can I apply if I'm studying abroad?

Yes, provided the foreign institution meets the scheme's ranking criteria.
